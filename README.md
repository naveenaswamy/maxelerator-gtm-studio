Opening

“Every accelerator has the same problem. Deeptech founders build incredible technology: quantum sensors, welding robots, AI that designs antibodies. But they have no go-to-market team. Nobody to figure out who buys first, how to price it, or how to get through a six-month enterprise sale.”

The idea

“So we built Maxelerator GTM Studio: an AI go-to-market team for every startup in the cohort. A program manager posts one message in BAND. Five AI agents pick it up, a strategist and four specialists, and together they write the startup’s playbook: who to sell to, what to say, how to price it, and a 90-day launch plan.”

The pricing model

“Pricing is the hardest part for deeptech, so we trained our own model for it. We took a tiny Qwen model, half a billion parameters, and trained it on my laptop in six minutes. Before training, its pricing was broken: it failed five checks and would have broken billing. After training, it produces valid pricing every time we tested it.”

The moment that matters

“But here’s the part I’m proudest of. In our live run, a startup called Weldbot joined: welding robots with a ten-week hardware lead time. Our small model suggested a twelve-week pilot. The agent caught it. It said: if the pilot clock starts at order, the robot arrives in week ten, and the customer gets two weeks of real use. And it refused to make up a better price. Instead, it asked the founder three questions. The founder answered: robots-as-a-service, four thousand dollars a robot per month, a pilot that starts at commissioning. A mentor approved it.”

Billing

“Once approved, that pricing becomes a real FlexPrice plan, ready for the startup to bill its customers. And every step of the studio is billed in credits from the startup’s wallet, so the accelerator knows exactly what each playbook costs.”

Why it’s safe

“Nothing touches real money without a human. The small model drafts, the agent checks, the founder fills the gaps, and a mentor approves.”

Close

“Maxelerator GTM Studio: a go-to-market team in a box, for every startup, from day one. The code is on GitHub, and I’m happy to show it live.”
